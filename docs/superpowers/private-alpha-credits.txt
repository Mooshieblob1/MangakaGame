Mangaka Studio — private alpha 0.8.0-private-alpha.9

Game direction and Helper-Chan references supplied by the project owner.
Helper-Chan portraits and the desk illustration use the supplied references;
expression variants and manga showcase atlases were generated for this project.
Customizable chibi employees, furniture and environments use original procedural geometry. Fixed Helper-Chan and parent models were generated and rigged with Tripo from the owner-supplied references and generated chibi adaptations. Their animation clips were authored locally for this project.
Room tone, pencil activity and soft interface effects are original procedural
audio authored for this project. No music or voice recordings are bundled.
