# NovelAI V5 art on the Steam store page and title screen: commercial terms

Date checked: 2026-10-04. Research only, no code change. Question from the
2026-10-04 open-work audit: may NovelAI Diffusion V5 images be used on the
Steam store page and the game's title screen?

## Answer

Yes. NovelAI's current Terms of Service give the user full ownership of what
they generate and put no limit on commercial use, attribution or where the
images may appear. Nothing extra needs to be bought or requested. Three things
still apply, and they come from elsewhere:

1. **Steam disclosure.** Valve requires the AI content disclosure on the
   Steamworks content survey whenever generated content ships in the game or
   its store page and marketing. NovelAI art on the title screen and capsules
   counts.
2. **Infringement check is ours.** NovelAI says outputs can resemble existing
   copyrighted work and that checking before commercial use is the user's job.
   Each kept image should get a quick look for anything recognisable (a known
   character, a real logo, readable brand text) before it goes on the store.
3. **Weak protection for the raw images.** Purely AI-generated images are
   generally not protected by copyright in the US (and likely elsewhere), so
   others could reuse the raw art. The typeset logo, the game itself and any
   meaningful human editing (compositing, paint-over, layout) are ours in the
   usual way. Not a blocker for using the art.

## What the terms say (facts, quoted)

Source: NovelAI Terms of Service, https://novelai.net/terms, "Effective:
December 8th, 2025", read 2026-10-04.

- **1.3 Ownership:** "You, whether a legal or physical entity, retain all
  rights and ownership of your Content. We do not claim any ownership rights
  to your Content."
- **1.4 Content:** "Content refers to the generated material that you
  specifically create using our Services. Your content is wholly yours and is
  not representative or affiliated with Anlatan".
- **10.3 Survivorship:** ownership is listed among the provisions that survive
  termination, so images made while subscribed stay ours after the
  subscription ends.
- **No commercial-use clause and no attribution clause.** The terms neither
  restrict nor mention commercial use of outputs. The restrictions in 5.3 and
  9.1 are about the service and the account (no reselling or sharing access,
  no bots, no circumventing limits), not about outputs.
- **5.2:** users must be 18 or older. **8.1:** no account sharing. Both are met
  as long as Blob generates the art on their own account.

Source: NovelAI documentation FAQ, https://docs.novelai.net/en/faq/, read
2026-10-04.

- "Who holds the copyright of my generated images? Can I monetize my generated
  images?": NovelAI "does not claim any ownership over the images you generate
  and waives any responsibility that arises from usage outside the service."
- "How does the AI handle copyright...": "it is possible for the AI to generate
  content that could be considered copyrighted material. If you intend on
  publicly sharing or commercializing whatever content you produce, please
  review your content."

Caution on secondary sources: a web search summary claimed "Section 5.2"
explicitly permits games, NFTs and publishing. That is wrong for the current
terms (5.2 is the age rule). The permission comes from 1.3 and 1.4 plus the
absence of any restriction.

## Steam's rules (facts)

Source: Game Developer, "Valve tweaks and clarifies AI disclosure rules for
Steam", Danielle Riendeau, 2026-01-16,
https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/valve-tweaks-and-clarifies-ai-disclosure-rules-for-steam

- Since the January 2026 rewrite, AI tools used only for workflow (coding
  assistants and the like) need no disclosure.
- Disclosure is needed when generative AI content ends up in the game or "the
  game's associated materials", which includes marketing assets and the store
  page. The developer describes the use in a text box on the content survey.
- Our disclosure will cover: NovelAI V5 (store art, title screen, logo mark),
  Suno (music), Tripo (3D models, if any trial assets ship). The in-game
  start-up disclaimer (Q37, Q39) already tells players.

## Copyright of AI images (context, not legal advice)

US Copyright Office, "Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2:
Copyrightability" (January 2025), and Thaler v. Perlmutter (D.C. Circuit,
March 2025): material generated by AI without sufficient human authorship is
not registrable; human selection, arrangement and modification can be. This
affects how well the raw art can be protected, not whether we may use it.
