## 3. The one live mark that shares a class: MANGAKA96 in the EU

**EUTM 019197807** ("MANGAKA96", owner Luca Molinaro, Italy) is the single most
important finding. It was filed on 4 June 2025, registered on 25 September 2025, and
expires in June 2035. It covers **Classes 9 and 41** across all 27 EU member states,
which are the same classes a Mangaka Days filing would target. The Class 9 goods are
described broadly as "Recorded and downloadable media, computer software..."; the
Class 41 services include "Entertainment services". The application reference
"Manga96-den" and the owner being a named individual (rather than a company) suggest
a personal creator project, but the registration is live, was unopposed, and carries
full EU protection.

The mark itself is "MANGAKA96", not "MANGAKA DAYS". An examiner at the EUIPO would
assess similarity on the dominant element, which is the shared word "MANGAKA". Because
"MANGAKA" is a Japanese common word (meaning "manga artist"), the examiner might
treat it as having lower distinctive character, reducing the likelihood that "MANGAKA
DAYS" would be blocked. However, this is not certain, and a clearance opinion from a
European trademark attorney is the only reliable answer. The mark was registered
without opposition, so no third party challenged it on distinctiveness grounds yet.

All other marks containing "MANGAKA" are in **Class 16 only** (Kuretake's art pens
and stationery, registered in Japan, Australia, EU, and China). Class 16 covers paper
goods and stationery, not software or entertainment services, so there is no direct
conflict with Classes 9 or 41 for a game product. A trademark examiner might still
note the existence of the Class 16 mark when assessing a Class 9 or 41 application,
but the goods are substantially different.

**MANGA STUDIO** (Celsys, USPTO Class 9, Reg. 3170199) is live and renewed, but the
full mark is different from "Mangaka Days" and the registered goods describe
comic-creation software, not a career simulation game. The Celsys mark's primary
market association is with Clip Studio Paint (formerly sold as Manga Studio in the
West), which further distinguishes it from a game.

## 4. No existing product or media uses this title

Across Steam, itch.io, Google Play, and general web search, no game, app, manga,
anime, light novel, or event uses the title "Mangaka Days" or its Japanese equivalent
"マンガカ・デイズ" / "漫画家デイズ". The closest Steam product by name is **"MangaKa"**
(app ID 2234500), a 3D pose-reference and line-art tool for illustrators released in
April 2023. It is one word (not two), in a different product category, and has no
career-simulation component. The closest product by concept is **"The Manga Works"**
(Kairosoft, 2022), which is a manga-artist career simulation with a clearly different
name. **"Mangaka Simulator"** and **"Manga Maker 2"** (Issak Company, Android) are
close in concept but differ in name. None of these creates a name conflict, though
they form the competitive landscape the game will launch into.

Kodansha operates a major manga-reading app called **"Comic Days"** (コミックDAYS,
2018) on Google Play and the web. This is a subscription reading service, not a game,
and "Comic Days" differs meaningfully from "Mangaka Days". A formal trademark
registration for "Comic Days" by Kodansha in Japan was not confirmed via J-PlatPat
automated search, though the search may have been incomplete (see gaps). Even if
Kodansha holds an unregistered or registered right in "Comic Days", the marks share
only the word "Days" and target different audiences entirely.

## 5. Domain and handle situation

**mangakadays.com and mangakadays.net** are both registered and parked on the Sedo
marketplace for sale. Neither is in active use as a website. This is consistent with
speculative domain registration rather than an active competing business. The domains
are available for purchase through Sedo negotiation. The **.jp** and **.gg** TLDs were
not directly checked; .jp requires a physical address in Japan managed by JPRS.

The social handle **@mangakadays** on X/Twitter returned a 402 status on direct fetch,
which is ambiguous (the account may not exist, or may require login to view). No
"mangakadays" account was found in web search results for Instagram, TikTok, or
YouTube. The handle **@mangadays** is taken on X/Twitter by an unrelated low-activity
Japanese manga-reading account, and **@mangadaysmaster** runs an unrelated manga
aggregation site, neither of which conflicts with a game under the name "Mangaka Days".

## 6. Bottom line and recommended next steps

The name "Mangaka Days" appears clean to use as a game title. No registered mark using
"MANGAKA DAYS" was found in any register searched. The name is unused across Steam,
mobile stores, manga and anime media, and general web search. The biggest registered
risk is **MANGAKA96 at the EUIPO** (Classes 9 and 41), which a European attorney must
assess before any EU trademark filing. Outside the EU, the space is clear in the
registers that were directly queried.

The following steps are recommended, separated by what is a verified fact and what is
inference.

**Facts established by this search.** "MANGAKA DAYS" is unregistered everywhere
searched. MANGAKA96 is a live EUTM in Classes 9 and 41. Kuretake holds "MANGAKA" in
Class 16 in Japan, Australia, the EU, and China (not Classes 9 or 41, not in the US
or Singapore). The .com and .net domains are parked for sale. Kodansha's "Comic Days"
formal registration status in Japan is unconfirmed.

**Inferred or uncertain.** Whether MANGAKA96 would block a "MANGAKA DAYS" EUTM
application is a legal judgment call, not a determination this search can make.
Singapore's register was not fully queried. The WIPO Global Brand Database was not
searched, leaving some Madrid-protocol filings unconfirmed.

**Recommended next steps.**

1. Before filing in the EU, engage a European trademark attorney to assess
   MANGAKA96 (EUTM 019197807) and advise whether a "MANGAKA DAYS" application in
   Classes 9 and 41 is viable, and whether MANGAKA96 is in commercial use (which
   affects potential revocation after five years of non-use).

2. Engage an Australian IP attorney (or use IP Australia's own TM Checker at
   ipaustralia.gov.au) to confirm the Class 9/41 picture in Australia, and to conduct
   a manual IPOS Singapore search, since automated access failed.

3. Engage a Japanese patent attorney (弁理士) to run a complete J-PlatPat search for
   Kodansha as applicant with any DAYS-related marks, and to advise on whether
   "マンガカ" (MANGAKA) as part of a Class 9/41 game title faces a descriptiveness
   objection under Japan Trademark Act Article 3.

4. Acquire the .com or .net domain via Sedo before launch. Both are parked, not
   actively used, and standard Sedo negotiation should resolve this. Optionally also
   check and register .gg (common for gaming services) and consider .io as an
   alternative.

5. Register @mangakadays on X/Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok as soon as
   possible to secure the handles before they are taken.

6. File for trademark protection in the intended launch markets at or before the Steam
   Early Access date, not after, to establish a priority date. At minimum this means
   Japan (JPO), Australia (IP Australia), and the US (USPTO), alongside the EU
   question. A Madrid Protocol application based on an Australian filing (IP Australia) could cover
   multiple markets from a single filing.
