# Sub-project 5: Historical timeline research ledger

Research date: 2026-09-24. Historical cutoff: 2025-12-31.
Status: production catalog evidence completed on 2026-09-24. Supported date
precision and deliberate simplifications are recorded below.
Related: [design](2026-09-24-historical-timeline-design.md).

## Evidence rules

Use publisher, creator, official adaptation, industry association or government
sources. A retrospective page can establish an earlier event; its current sales
total cannot establish how popular a title was at launch. Do not incorporate
post-2025 developments just because a live source now includes them.

Keep each historical claim's source, supported date precision and interpretation
separate from its game trigger. Magazine issue labels, cover dates, actual sale
dates, book publication dates and digital republication dates are different.
Never convert issue 12 into March 12 or treat a collected volume's release as
the original serialization's final chapter date.

The exact game hour, analogue names, magazine mappings, salaries, recruitability,
talent, popularity curves and demand modifiers below are design choices. They
are not measurements of real creators or Japanese employment practices.

## Historical assistant opportunities

The player employing these people is necessarily fictional. A historical launch
can establish a latest departure boundary; it cannot establish an actual job
vacancy, willingness to work for the player, salary or available dates.

- **Eiichido Oga**, inspired by One Piece's creator: proposed availability from
  the April 1996 game start, fixed departure 1 April 1997. The July 1997 series
  launch is supported above. This particular one-year employment opportunity
  and three-month preparation buffer are explicitly fictional. The current
  evidence does not establish his real contractual availability in 1996.
- **Tatsuya Enno**, inspired by Spy x Family's creator: proposed availability
  January–December 2006, fixed departure 1 January 2007. In a [publisher-hosted
  creator/editor interview](https://sp.shonenjump.com/p/sp/million-tag/dialog/1/),
  Endo identifies TISTA as his 2007 serialization debut and describes assistant
  work between serializations. That supports the distinction between early
  work and the much later 2019 hit; it does not verify a 2006 assistant contract.

Use these as two bounded initial gameplay opportunities, not a complete creator
biography system. Their skills and mentoring are fictional balance profiles.
Do not make a creator available immediately before their best-known hit if
earlier serializations already conflict. Further analogues require an explicit
career-source check, not a blanket "two years before their hit" rule.

Additional primary evidence closes the initial ledger's gaps:

- [Shueisha's BLEACH JET release](https://prtimes.jp/a/?c=35304&f=d35304-2-pdf-0.pdf&r=2), page 3, explicitly supplies 7 August 2001 and 22 August 2016. Its adaptation-month text conflicts with the anime's own history; use the anime source for October 2004.
- [BE LOVE September 2022](https://be-love.jp/bn/2022/9.html) explicitly advertises the final Chihayafuru chapter and 1 August on-sale date.
- [Haikyu official introduction](https://haikyu.jp/) establishes February 2012. [Shueisha's final-chapter merchandise correction](https://www.shonenjump.com/j/notice/2020/11/26/201126_oshirase001.html) identifies the final issue as the 20 July 2020 issue 33/34.
- [Demon Slayer's licensed stage introduction](https://kimetsu.com/stage/2021/about/) states serialization continued through May 2020. The initial catalog retains year precision for the 2016 launch; July is a visibly approximate trigger, not the real launch day.
- [Shueisha's 23 December 2025 notice](https://books.shueisha.co.jp/info/comics/3861.html) explicitly describes One Piece as currently serialized. Only that contemporary status is used; the notice's announced 2026 adaptation dates are excluded.
- [Shogakukan's 24 December 2025 issue](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/magazine?cd=2066301126) lists Conan chapter 1156 and a Frieren absence. [Its 22 October issue](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/magazine?cd=2066111125) also lists Frieren as absent, after chapter 147 in the [15 October issue](https://shogakukan-comic.jp/magazine?cd=2066510125). The game begins its modeled hiatus on the first subsequent issue date. The official social announcement could not be fetched directly, so catalog evidence uses the publisher's accessible issue records.
- [Jump+ chapter 126](https://shonenjumpplus.com/episode/17107094912731879193) is dated 8 December 2025 and establishes late-2025 original Spy x Family publication. Current page widgets for 2026 books are excluded.

The four individually sourced adaptation milestones are Naruka (2002, year),
Blanch (October 2004), Demon Lantern (2019, year) and After the Hero (September
2023). They use the primary sources in the initial table. These dates anchor
fictional demand ramps; they do not claim measured sales gains. Other rivals
use a disclosed generic one-year growth delay rather than invented adaptation
history. The 2025 electronic-comics report triggers in July, after publication.

No quantitative piracy-to-lost-sales conversion, streaming-demand coefficient,
salary history or studio adoption cost was established by this research. Such
values must remain explicit gameplay estimates, or be omitted. Do not hold the
basic timeline implementation hostage to an unsupported causal model.
