# Art commission brief (logo, store art, title screen)

Date: 2026-09-28. The shareable brief for outside artists and designers is
published as a private page: https://claude.ai/artifact/GEhhamaa47ej99CvVXrhaM
(the user shares it from the page's Share menu). This file records what it asks
for, so later work stays consistent with it.

**Change of plan (2026-09-28):** the user intends to commission the logo, the
Steam store images and the title screen art from a designer, instead of
generating the store art themselves as planned in Tier 2 question 7. The piece
list and content rules still follow the [store art brief](store-art-brief.md).

- **Scope:** (1) logo, (2) one key illustration, (3) every Steam capsule, banner,
  background and icon adapted from it, (4) the game's title screen background.
  Quotes for the package or per part.
- **Helper-Chan leads everything**, from the approved reference
  ([helper-chan.png](references/helper-chan.png)): very long blonde drill
  twintails with black ties, black rectangular glasses, blue eyes, white blouse,
  red lanyard with ID badge, dark pencil skirt, clipboard. Full-proportion style
  in the key art; a simplified mascot version is welcome in the logo and icons.
  Confident and professional, nothing suggestive.
- **Setting:** a typical, lived-in late-1990s Tokyo mangaka studio: tilted desks
  with blue-line manuscript pages, dip pens and ink, screentone, reference books,
  a deadline calendar, a beige CRT and fax, and a window onto a Tokyo street in
  evening light. Helper-Chan in front with her clipboard; behind her an empty
  drawing desk with a half-inked page and the pen set down, the player's seat.
- **No player character (2026-09-28):** the mangaka never appears in the art,
  because players design their own; Aki is only the default name.
- **Name (decided 2026-09-28): Mangaka Days**, replacing the working title
  "Mangaka Studio", which was one letter from "Manga Studio" (the old Western
  name of Clip Studio Paint). No game, app or domain of that name was found on
  Steam, itch.io, Google Play or the web; a formal trademark search is still the
  user's to do (USPTO, WIPO, J-PlatPat, and IP Australia, the user's home
  office). The mark and wordmark stay separable (the mark alone for icons) and
  the logo stays on its own layer; "Days" may appear as a sun and moon, calendar
  page or day-to-dusk colour, as an invitation only.
- **Pieces:** logo master and library logo (1280 wide or 720 high); header 920 x
  430, small 462 x 174, main 1232 x 706, vertical 748 x 896, library 600 x 900
  and library header capsules; library hero 3840 x 1240 (no text); store page
  background 1438 x 810; event cover 800 x 450 and header 1920 x 622; icons 184,
  256 and 32 px; title screen background 3840 x 2160 master that crops to 16:9 and
  21:9 with space for the logo and menu, plus the title screen logo. Store
  screenshots are captured from the game, not commissioned.
- **Deliverables:** layered vector logo plus outlined version; transparent logo
  PNGs (full, wordmark, mark; dark, light and one-colour); layered key art source;
  every piece as an exact-size PNG; colour values and font licences.
- **Process:** logo concepts and a key art sketch, one direction each, two
  revision rounds, then adaptation to the full set with one revision round.
- **Rights:** full exclusive use in the game, stores, trailers, marketing and
  merchandise, copyright transfer preferred; portfolio use after announcement;
  the artist says whether they use generative AI, since the game discloses AI
  content.

A shorter brief for VGen illustrators, key art and logo only with the user
cropping the capsules, is in [vgen-capsule-brief.md](vgen-capsule-brief.md).

Until the designed logo and title art exist, the title screen uses a placeholder
built in the game (see the title screen design).
