# VGen commission brief: key art and logo

Date: 2026-09-28. A short brief for anime-style illustrators on VGen: one key
art illustration and a logo only. The user places the logo and crops every
Steam capsule themselves. The full package brief (logo, key art, every Steam
image and the title screen) is the [art commission brief](logo-designer-brief.md).
Capsule sizes were checked against the Steamworks store and library asset pages
on 2026-09-28; see the [store art brief](store-art-brief.md).

**Commission request: key art and logo for Mangaka Days (Steam)**

Hi! I'm making Mangaka Days, a cosy management game for PC. You guide a young manga artist through a career in late-1990s Tokyo, from drawing self-published comics at their parents' house to running their own studio. It's heading to Steam Early Access, and I'd love your help with the store art.

**What I'm commissioning**

1. **Key art.** One illustration with a clear focal point: Helper-Chan, the game's mascot and the player's assistant, in a cramped, lived-in mangaka studio at dusk. Please keep her on her own layer.
2. **Logo.** "Mangaka Days" as a transparent PNG or vector, in a wide layout and a stacked layout, with versions for light and dark backgrounds.

I'll place the logo and crop all the Steam sizes myself.

**Helper-Chan:** an adult office worker with very long blonde twintails ending in big drill curls (black ties), black rectangular glasses, blue eyes, a white blouse, a red lanyard with an ID badge, a dark pencil skirt, and a clipboard she always carries. Confident and friendly, fully clothed, nothing suggestive. Reference sheet below.

Please don't draw the player character, since players design their own. An empty drawing desk with a half-inked page works nicely as "their" seat.

**Making it read as manga at a glance:** a tilted drawing desk with manuscript pages, dip pens and an ink bottle, screentone textures, ink splashes, and panel borders or speed lines behind her. Late-90s touches are welcome too: a beige CRT, a fax machine, a window onto overhead power lines.

**It's for Steam capsules**, so please compose for thumbnails: one focal point, strong contrast, big clear shapes, and no small details that matter.

**Files**

- Layered PSD or CSP, with the character, background and effects on separate layers.
- Roughly square, about 4000 x 4000 px, with generous extra background on every edge. I'll crop it very wide (about 3:1) and tall (about 2:3), so please keep her face and the key details near the centre and leave clear space for the logo.

**Process:** a rough sketch first, please, so we can fix the composition before you render. One round of changes at the sketch stage and one at the colour stage would be perfect.

**Usage rights:** this is commercial work. I'd use it on the Steam store page, in trailers, on social media and possibly on merch. Could you confirm in writing that this is okay, and let me know if commercial or merch use is priced separately? I also need to declare AI content on Steam, so please confirm the work is drawn by you without generative AI tools.

**Budget:** about US$500 to $800 for both pieces, including commercial use. If your quote is different, I'm happy to talk it through.

**Deadline:** no fixed launch date yet. I'd love a sketch within about 2 weeks of you starting and the finished files within about 6 weeks, but I can work around your queue.
