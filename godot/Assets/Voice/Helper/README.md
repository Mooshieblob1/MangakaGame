Helper-Chan voice lines

Generated 2026-10-10 with ElevenLabs Eleven v4 (Pay As You Go, a paid plan that
grants commercial use) from "Helper-Chan", a Voice Design voice the project owner
made on the ElevenLabs website (thick Japanese accent, flustered, early 20s).

Each line was written as quoted IPA phrase blocks, the format the owner chose
after listening tests (bare per-word IPA and quoted per-word IPA were also tried;
quoted per-word made her pause after every word). Example, `tea.ogg`:

  [quietly, speaking English with a thick Japanese accent] "/a poːzɯ izɯ aɾaɯdo/".
  [small laugh] "/ai tʃekːɯdo/". [tender] "/aiɾɯ stei bisaido jɯ/".
  "/wi donto habɯ tɯ fiɾɯ za saiɾensɯ/".

Settings: model eleven_v4, default voice settings, seed 1111. goal-legend was
remade the same day because Blob heard it wrong ("guzzu" for merchandise and a
blurred "will remember"): it now says "/maːtʃandaizɯ/" and is seed 4444, the
one of four takes Scribe heard word for word. Converted with ffmpeg: loudnorm I=-18 LUFS, TP=-1.5 dB,
mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis q5. The source MP3s, Scribe transcripts and the other
takes are kept outside the repository on the owner's PC
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\MangakaGame\voice-tests\2026-10-10).
